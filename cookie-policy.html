Last updated: July 30, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how InvoiceMate Global uses cookies and similar technologies to recognize you when you visit our website at invoicemateglobal.com.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small data files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently and provide analytics reporting.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Necessary for core website operation, such as remembering your privacy preferences and cookie settings.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our website so we can improve performance and user experience.

3. Managing Cookie Preferences

You can adjust or withdraw your consent to cookies at any time using the Cookie Consent banner on our website or through your web browser settings.
