Product Changelog

Track continuous improvements, new country fiscal presets, localization updates, and offline PDF engine enhancements across InvoiceMate Global.

v1.5.0 Latest Release
September 2026

Multi-Country Fiscal Profiles & Portuguese Language Support

This milestone update expands InvoiceMate Global's regional fiscal compliance automation, adds full Portuguese localization, and enhances the vector PDF rendering pipeline.

✨ What's New

  • ✓
    Business Settings Automation: Country selection now automatically updates tax registration headers, standard rate toggles, and localized tax number formats (including US EIN, Indian GSTIN, European VAT/TVA/MwSt., and Portuguese NIF).
  • ✓
    Automatic country fiscal presets for Portugal (EUR, IVA, NIF): Automatically configures standard Portuguese VAT tiers (23% normal, 13% intermédio, 6% reduzido), currency formatting, and formal NIF (Número de Identificação Fiscal) display fields.
  • ✓
    Native Portuguese language translation (values-pt): Full end-to-end interface localization in Portuguese (Português) across all screens, modals, status labels, and generated invoice document headers.
  • ✓
    UTF-8 compliance update in the PDF generator engine: Enhanced font rendering and character encoding support for special accents (ã, õ, ç, á, é, í), international currency glyphs, and multilingual business names.
  • ✓
    JSON Schema Backup & Expenses Validation: Web platform import/export validator now fully recognizes countryCode, taxIdLabel, taxIdValue, and expenses schemas from Android device backups, ensuring imported backups or clean wipes match device states identically.
  • ✓
    Data Management & Factory Reset Alignment: Standardized local purge routines ensuring that performing a Factory Reset completely purges all local data from your device, including Invoices, Credit Notes, Clients, Inventory Items, Payments, and Tracked Expenses, resetting all dashboard metric counters to 0.00.
